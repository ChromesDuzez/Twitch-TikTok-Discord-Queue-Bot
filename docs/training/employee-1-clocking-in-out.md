# Employee Video 1 — Your Time Clock: Clocking In & Out

## At a glance
- **Audience:** every employee.
- **Length target:** 3–4 minutes.
- **Have on screen before recording:** the demo employee's own **clock channel** with
  their time-clock message visible and currently **clocked out**.
- **Goal:** by the end, the employee can find their clock, clock in, clock out, and knows
  what the little status icons mean.

## Outline
1. Where your time clock lives.
2. Clocking in (and what "pending approval" means).
3. The status icons on your clock.
4. Lunch — the quick version.
5. Clocking out.
6. One rule: end your work before you clock out (teaser for Video 2).

## Written guide (pairs with the video)

**Your time clock**
Every employee has a personal **time clock** — a message with buttons in your own channel.
You clock in and out by pressing buttons; you never have to type anything.

**Clock in**
1. Open your clock channel and find your time-clock message.
2. Press the green **Clock-In** button. Your start time is recorded immediately.
3. The clock updates to show you're on the clock.

**About approvals (you might not see this)**
- Some accounts clock in instantly; others show a **pending** clock icon (🕓) until a
  manager approves the punch. **Either way your time is recorded.**
- If a punch stays pending for a long time, tell your manager.

**Status icons**
- ✅ approved  ·  🕓 pending approval  ·  ☁️ synced to the office system  ·  🟡 still clocked in.
- These are just status — you don't need to act on them.

**Lunch (quick version)**
A 30-minute lunch is deducted automatically once your shift reaches six hours. The
**Ignoring Lunch Break** button is only for a few managers — ignore it.

**Clock out**
1. Come back to the same clock at the end of your day.
2. If you see the red **Clock-Out** button, press it — your end time is recorded.
3. If you instead see **End Work** buttons, you still have a job running. Finish it first
   (see *Video 2 — Logging Your Work*), then press **Clock-Out**.
