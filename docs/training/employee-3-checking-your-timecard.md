# Employee Video 3 — Checking Your Timecard (and Fixing Problems)

## At a glance
- **Audience:** every employee.
- **Length target:** 3–4 minutes.
- **Have on screen before recording:** the demo employee with a few days of punches +
  worktime already in the current week, and DMs open with the bot.
- **Goal:** by the end, the employee can pull up their own timecard, read it, run a custom
  range, fix a stuck clock, and knows the right way to get a mistake corrected.

## Outline
1. See your week with **/mytimecard**.
2. How to read it (days, hours, totals).
3. A custom date range with **/timecardrange**.
4. If your clock looks stuck: the **Refresh** button and **/refreshclock**.
5. If something's wrong: tell a manager (what to tell them).

## Written guide (pairs with the video)

**Check your week**
- Type **/mytimecard** — leave the date blank for this week, or enter a **Saturday** date
  for an older week. The bot DMs you your timecard (or shows it privately in the channel if
  your DMs are closed).
- It shows each day, your clock-in/out times, each punch's length, your logged jobs, and
  your **weekly totals** (hours clocked and hours after lunch).

**Custom date range**
- Type **/timecardrange** and give a **start** and **end** date (up to one year) for a
  specific period like a pay period or a month.

**If your clock looks stuck**
- Press the **Refresh** button on your clock message to redraw it, or
- Type **/refreshclock** to rebuild the message if it's missing or broken.

**If something's wrong — tell a manager**
The timecard is the official record. Don't work around a mistake yourself. Report it with:
- the **day**,
- the rough **time**, and
- **what should have happened** (e.g., "forgot to clock out ~4:30 Tuesday," or "that
  service call should've been the Smith job").

Managers have the tools to correct punches and work entries cleanly.
