# Employee Video 2 — Logging Your Work: Construction, Service & Office

## At a glance
- **Audience:** every employee.
- **Length target:** 4–6 minutes.
- **Have on screen before recording:** the demo employee **clocked in** (so the Start
  buttons are showing). Have a known demo customer/job ready to search for.
- **Goal:** by the end, the employee can start work on a job, pick the right
  customer/job, and end that work — and understands Shop time is automatic.

## Outline
1. Why we log work (the three kinds).
2. Start **Construction** — pick the customer's job.
3. Start **Service** — pick the field-service task.
4. Start **Office** — no customer needed.
5. Ending work: **Now** vs **Custom**.
6. Only one job at a time; switching jobs.
7. "Shop" time is automatic — you don't press anything for it.
8. Then clock out.

## Written guide (pairs with the video)

**The three kinds of work**
While clocked in you log what you're doing against a job:
- **Construction** — a customer's construction job.
- **Service** — a field-service call for a customer.
- **Office** — desk work at the shop (no customer).

**Start a job**
1. While clocked in, press **Start Construction**, **Start Service**, or **Start Office**.
2. For Construction/Service: type the **customer's name** and pick their job/task from the
   list (these come from the office system).
3. For Office: nothing to pick — it starts right away.
4. The clock now shows **End Work** buttons while the job runs.

**End a job**
- **End … Work Now** — the bot calculates your time from when you started, rounded to the
  nearest quarter hour. Use this most of the time.
- **End … Work Custom** — you type the number of hours. Use this if you forgot to start on
  time or need to log a specific amount.

**Rules of thumb**
- Only **one job runs at a time** — end the current one before starting another.
- Switching jobs = end the first, start the next.
- **Shop time is automatic.** Any clocked-in time not logged to a job becomes Shop on the
  report — there's no button for it.

**Finish your day**
End any running job, then press **Clock-Out** (from Video 1).
